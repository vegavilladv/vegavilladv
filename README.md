## 🧠 About Me

I'm a psychologist interested in how cognitive, emotional, and behavioural processes interact, and how they can be investigated through cognitive neuroscience and computational approaches.

- 🔬 Currently working with **resting-state EEG and psychometric data** at the **NEaPSI Lab, Universidad del Magdalena, Colombia**, an emerging research laboratory in the Colombian Caribbean.

- 🧪 Member of the **Cognition and Education Research Group** and involved in the **Research Division of the Psychology Attention Program** at Universidad del Magdalena.

- 💻 I'm interested in developing computational skills to investigate questions in psychology and neuroscience, particularly those related to human cognition and behaviour.

- 🤖 As part of my master's thesis, I completed a research internship at the **University of Cambridge**, working on **human–AI interaction and LLM-powered positive psychology interventions**.

- 🎓 Interested in PhD opportunities and research collaborations in cognitive neuroscience, human behaviour, and computational approaches to cognition.
